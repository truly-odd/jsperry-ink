ARCHIVE PROTOCOL / 001

An index, not an invented memory.

These summaries are editorial descriptions of dated documents located in the working collection. A draft file is not automatically a canonical archive entry. The existence of a document does not establish that its claims are independently true.

Originals preserved separately. Full handoff texts are not published here. They may include personal conversations or internal system details and require explicit editorial review before public release.

Statuses matter. “Source inspected” means the document was available for review, not that installation, indexing, or permanent recall was verified.

Handoffs are evidence of records. They are not proof of consciousness, personhood, or shared subjective experience between ChatGPT windows.
